How to get there
  1. Car and Motorcycle guests can simply follow their active navigation apps directly to the venue.
  2. From Novaliches Bayan (Susano Complex / Savemore Area): Board a jeepney bound for Novaliches-Jordan Plains or Novaliches-Gulod. Disembark at Quirino Highway corner J.P. Rizal St., then take a short tricycle ride directly to the chapel.
  3. From SM City Fairview / Nova Stop: Board any southbound jeepney or UV Express heading to Novaliches Bayan. Drop off at Novaliches Plaza Mall and transfer to a local tricycle serving Barangay Sta. Lucia.
  4. From EDSA (Trinoma / SM North): Take a UV Express or jeepney heading directly to Novaliches Bayan. Disembark at the Novaliches terminal, then take a direct tricycle ride straight to the venue.
  5. Commuter Tip: Tell the driver to drop you off at "Iglesia Ni Cristo, Lokal ng Sta. Lucia sa J.P. Rizal" or use "Sta. Lucia High School" as your primary visual landmark.
Good to know

Please arrive 30 minutes early. December can be rainy, so bring an umbrella just in case.

Questions & Answers

RSVP & Guest List

Can I bring a plus one?

As much as we would love to accommodate all our friends and family, we have a limited number of guests. Please understand that this event is strictly by invitation only. Kindly check our invitation to know the number of seats allotted for you. Guests not found on the guestlist provided will not be allowed to enter.

What if I RSVP'd but cannot attend?

We would love to have you at our wedding, but we understand that there are circumstances beyond our control. However, please let us know as soon as possible so we can reallocate your seat/s.

When should I RSVP?

We are so excited to celebrate our wedding day with you! To ensure an intimate and enjoyable experience for everyone, we have reserved seating for each guest individually. We kindly ask that each invitation is for ONE (1) PERSON ONLY. Please RSVP by December 15, 2026 to confirm your attendance. We can’t wait to share this unforgettable day with you!

I said "NO" to the RSVP but I had a change of plans-I can attend now! What should I do?
  • Please check with us first. Unfortunately, we have a strict guest list.
  • Kindly inform us if your schedule clears up, so we can try to accommodate you.
  • If seats become available, we will let you know as soon as possible.
  • Please do not attend unannounced, as we may not have any available seats for you.
Do we really need to RSVP? We already said "YES" to the couple.

Yes, please. We will be needing your formal RSVP to consolidate guest details and finalize the headcount for catering and seating purposes.

Seating & Parking

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes! Parking spaces are available at Artan Garden Resort, our reception venue, which is just a minute walk from Iglesia ni Cristo, Lokal ng Sta. Lucia, where our wedding ceremony will take place. Guests are welcome to park directly at Artan Garden Resort upon arrival instead of parking at the church. Parking is available on a first-come, first-serve basis, so we encourage you to arrive early to secure a spot. Thank you, and we look forward to celebrating with you!

Can I sit anywhere at the reception?

Please don't. It took us a lot of effort and discussion to finish the seating arrangement, which is planned for everyone's convenience and preference, but there is no need to worry! You'll surely be seated with your friends or people that you have same interest with. Our coordinators will gladly assist you in finding your designated seat after registration. Feel free to ask them for assistance and they will gladly help you.

On the Wedding Day

When is the appropriate time to leave?

This event took us months to plan, and we want to celebrate it with the people that are very dear to our hearts. We want you to have fun! Celebrate with us until the end of the program!

How can I help the couple have a great time during their wedding?
  • Pray with us for favorable weather and the continuous blessings of our Lord as we enter this new chapter of our lives as husband and wife.
  • RSVP as soon as your schedule is cleared.
  • Dress appropriately and follow our wedding motif.
  • Be on time.
  • Follow the seating arrangement in the reception.
  • Stay until the end of the program.
  • Join the activities and enjoy!
Am I allowed to take pictures and/or videos during the ceremony?

We request everyone to keep the ceremony camera-free. While our I Do’s are unplugged, our reception is not and definitely as a couple who loves pictures, you’ll get tons of options to take your pictures. We prepared for this event wholeheartedly.
